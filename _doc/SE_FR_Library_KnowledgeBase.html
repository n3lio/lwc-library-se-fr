Library summary

The CCO FR Component Library contains 35 components organized in 7 categories, built by the Solution Engineering France team. All components are industry-neutral, bilingual (FR/EN), and drop on any Salesforce demo org.

Category definitions

  • Transverse (8) — Cross-persona components usable on any page or object (Activity Feed, Record Highlights, Banners, Related Cards, Contact Card, Files Gallery, Contacts Carousel).
  • Productivity (5) — Task management, calendar, AI prompts, recommendations and timelines — tools that make any rep more efficient.
  • Sales (5) — Account-centric sales components: health score, strategy plan, pipeline, order entry, customer orders.
  • Service (5) — Case and service-oriented components: kanban, alerts, order summary, service contracts, subscribers list.
  • Marketing (4) — Marketing and Data Cloud components: segments, consent, engagement history, customer sales summary.
  • Dashboards & KPIs (5) — Full-page home dashboards and metric tiles for Field Sales, Telesales, and management.
  • Geo & Field (3) — Map and geolocation components for field reps: nearby accounts, territory map, voice notes.
